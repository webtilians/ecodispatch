WHY THIS EXISTS

From theorem to measurable experiment.

The goal is not to claim that a new theorem instantly solves emergency response. The goal is to create a reproducible test bed where each mathematical idea can be implemented, compared against baselines and kept only when it produces a measurable improvement.
