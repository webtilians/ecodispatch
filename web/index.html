MODEL BOUNDARY

A benchmark, not an emergency service.

This simulator is sequential and synthetic. Travel uses Euclidean distance, resources become immediately available after service, and the exact offline optimum is restricted to at most ten generated incidents. These assumptions are explicit so later versions can replace them one by one with road graphs, service durations, simultaneous incidents and real risk data.
